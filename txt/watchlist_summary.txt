=== AAOI ===

The company continues to prioritize scaling production capacity to improve margins, yet it carries a substantial debt load that necessitates sustained execution.

THEME: The stock is primarily driven by the massive expansion of AI infrastructure and the transition to 400G/800G optical networking components. Its strategic shift toward high-speed transceivers for major cloud service providers makes it a direct, albeit speculative, play on the ongoing data center upgrade cycle.

Investors are betting on the company's ability to displace traditional silicon-based power solutions in advanced military hardware.

=== ALB ===
